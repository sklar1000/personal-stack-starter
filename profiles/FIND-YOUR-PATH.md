# Find Your Path

Answer 3 questions. Get a personalized starting point.

---

## Question 1: What frustrates you most about how you work right now?

Pick the ONE that resonates most:

| | Frustration |
|---|---|
| **A** | I can never find what I need when I need it |
| **B** | AI doesn't actually help me — it's generic and forgetful |
| **C** | I feel scattered — too many things, nothing moves forward |
| **D** | I've tried organizing systems before and they all fall apart |
| **E** | I know what I should do but I can't make myself do it consistently |
| **F** | I rebuild the same frameworks from scratch for every project |

## Question 2: How do you currently use AI?

| | Level |
|---|---|
| **A** | I don't really — it feels like a novelty |
| **B** | I chat with it but re-explain myself every time |
| **C** | I've set up custom instructions or GPTs |
| **D** | I use Claude Code, Cursor, or similar tools for building |

## Question 3: How many projects or roles do you juggle?

| | Scale |
|---|---|
| **A** | Mostly one focused role or project |
| **B** | 2-3 roles (teach + consult, write + build, etc.) |
| **C** | 4+ roles or a "portfolio career" |

## Your Path

Find your combination below. Don't overthink it — you can always change direction.

---

### "I'm just curious what this is" (Q2: A or B, Q3: A)

**You are: The Explorer**

You don't need a system yet. You need to see what's possible.

**Start**: [Track A: Just Exploring](../01-foundation/TRACK-A-EXPLORE.md)
**Time**: 15 minutes
**Goal**: Talk to Claude with context for one week. See if the lightbulb goes on.

---

### "I have a project and I want to move faster" (Q1: A or C, Q2: any, Q3: A or B)

**You are: The Builder**

You have something specific to build and you want AI that actually helps.

**Start**: [Track B: Ready to Build](../01-foundation/TRACK-B-BUILD.md)
**Time**: 30 minutes
**Goal**: Working system with one project tracked, sessions bracketed, git history recording your progress.
**Then**: After 2 weeks, add [Tier 2: Workflow](../02-workflow/UPGRADE.md) for task management and frontmatter.

---

### "I want AI that actually knows me" (Q1: B or E, Q2: B or C, Q3: any)

**You are: The Personalizer**

You've used AI but it feels shallow. You want depth.

**Start**: [Track B: Ready to Build](../01-foundation/TRACK-B-BUILD.md) — but spend extra time on the "About Me" and "How I Work" sections of CLAUDE.md. This is where the magic happens. Write your values, your shadow patterns, your communication preferences. The more honest you are, the better Claude gets.
**Time**: 30 minutes (but take 45 if you want to go deep on identity)
**Then**: Add [Tier 2: Workflow](../02-workflow/UPGRADE.md) for session continuity. Consider the [Self Layer guide](../03-system/UPGRADE.md) for building a full identity file.

---

### "I need to stop context-switching and focus" (Q1: C, Q2: any, Q3: B or C)

**You are: The Operator**

You're juggling too much. You need constraints, not more tools.

**Start**: [Track B: Ready to Build](../01-foundation/TRACK-B-BUILD.md) — and pay special attention to the Focus limit in the governance rules. The constraint IS the feature: max 3 active projects. Everything else goes to Background or Paused.
**Time**: 30 minutes
**Then**: [Tier 2: Workflow](../02-workflow/UPGRADE.md) immediately — you'll need the project lifecycle tiers and governance rules from day one.

---

### "I'm a portfolio professional — I teach, consult, write, and build" (Q1: F, Q2: C or D, Q3: C)

**You are: The System Builder**

You don't just need a stack. You need knowledge architecture. Methodology separated from delivery. Frameworks that compound across projects.

**Start**: [Track B: Ready to Build](../01-foundation/TRACK-B-BUILD.md)
**Time**: 30 minutes for foundation, then plan a half-day within your first week
**Then**: Move through [Tier 2](../02-workflow/UPGRADE.md) and [Tier 3](../03-system/UPGRADE.md) fast. You'll want canon separation, multi-project structure, and automation. Read the [Design Patterns](../reference/design-patterns.md) early — it'll save you from reinventing architecture.

---

### "I've built elaborate systems before and they always rot" (Q1: D, Q2: C or D, Q3: any)

**You are: The Rebuilder**

You've done this dance before. The problem isn't you — it's that every system you've tried demanded discipline you couldn't sustain. You need a system that compensates for the discipline gap, not one that punishes it.

**Start**: [Track B: Ready to Build](../01-foundation/TRACK-B-BUILD.md) — but here's the key: START SMALL. One project. Three commands. Don't build the elaborate version on day one. Let it grow from use, not from planning.
**Time**: 30 minutes (resist the urge to make it 3 hours)
**Then**: [Tier 2](../02-workflow/UPGRADE.md) adds session bracketing and governance — the self-maintaining parts that keep it alive when you don't show up.

---

## Still Not Sure?

Start with [Track A](../01-foundation/TRACK-A-EXPLORE.md). You can't break anything. Try it for a week. If it clicks, move to Track B. If it doesn't, you lost 15 minutes.
