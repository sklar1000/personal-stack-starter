# Personal Stack Maturity Model

Where you are, where you're going, and how to know when you've arrived at each level.

---

## The 7 Levels

Each level builds on the previous. You don't skip levels — but you can move through them fast.

---

### Level 1: Structured Vault

**What you have**: A folder with organized files. CLAUDE.md at the root.

**What it gives you**: One place for everything. AI that knows your context.

**You're here when**:
- All your files are in one place (not scattered across apps)
- Claude reads your CLAUDE.md and knows who you are
- You can find what you need in under 30 seconds

**Entry cost**: 5 folders, 1 file, 30 minutes.

**Tier in this repo**: [Track A](../01-foundation/TRACK-A-EXPLORE.md) or [Track B](../01-foundation/TRACK-B-BUILD.md)

---

### Level 2: AI Co-Pilot

**What you add**: Identity file, project context, session bookends.

**What it gives you**: AI that doesn't just know your files — it knows YOU. Sessions have continuity.

**You're here when**:
- Claude's responses feel personalized, not generic
- You don't re-explain yourself between sessions
- `/start-session` gives you a useful briefing

**Entry cost**: Identity file + CLAUDE.md enrichment, 1 hour.

**Tier in this repo**: [Track B](../01-foundation/TRACK-B-BUILD.md) + [Tier 2](../02-workflow/UPGRADE.md)

---

### Level 3: Command System

**What you add**: Slash commands for repeating workflows. 5-10 commands.

**What it gives you**: Complex processes reduced to one word. Consistency without memory.

**You're here when**:
- You have 5+ commands that you actually use
- New workflows get encoded as commands, not remembered
- You'd feel the loss if the commands disappeared

**Entry cost**: First 3-5 commands, 2 hours over a week.

**Tier in this repo**: [Tier 2](../02-workflow/UPGRADE.md) + [Tier 3](../03-system/UPGRADE.md)

---

### Level 4: Knowledge Governance

**What you add**: Canon separation — methodology separated from delivery. Versioned frameworks.

**What it gives you**: Knowledge that compounds across projects instead of getting rebuilt.

**You're here when**:
- You have at least one "canonical" framework you reference across projects
- Frameworks have version numbers
- Updating the source updates everything downstream

**Entry cost**: Canon separation exercise, half-day.

**Tier in this repo**: [Tier 3](../03-system/UPGRADE.md)

---

### Level 5: Active Maintenance

**What you add**: Governance rules, escalation tiers, self-monitoring.

**What it gives you**: A system that notices when things are stale and nudges you. Works whether you show up or not.

**You're here when**:
- The system tells you what's stale without you asking
- Ignored items escalate on a schedule
- You come back after a break and the system knows what needs attention

**Entry cost**: Escalation tiers + governance rules, 2 hours.

---

### Level 6: Connected System

**What you add**: Cross-project discovery, entity relationships, semantic search.

**What it gives you**: Your AI finds connections you didn't know existed. A framework from one project surfaces in another.

**You're here when**:
- Claude references things from other projects that are relevant
- You discover connections between ideas you hadn't seen
- The system surprises you with useful context

---

### Level 7: Self-Extending

**What you add**: The system builds its own capabilities. Friction → command → automation.

**What it gives you**: A system that grows from use. Every annoyance becomes a feature.

**You're here when**:
- You regularly turn friction into new commands or rules
- The system today is meaningfully different from the system 30 days ago
- Building system improvements feels as natural as using the system

---

## Where Most People Land

| Profile | Typical Ceiling | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Personal use, one project** | Level 2-3 | Enough to be useful. No need for governance. |
| **Professional, 2-3 projects** | Level 3-4 | Commands + some knowledge separation. |
| **Portfolio career, teaching + building** | Level 5-6 | Full governance + cross-project discovery. |
| **System builder (like Michael)** | Level 7 | The system IS the practice. |

**There's no wrong level.** A Level 2 stack that you use daily beats a Level 7 stack that you abandoned. Match complexity to need.
